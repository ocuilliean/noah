About Us

Bethelema Prophetic Ministry is a resurgence of truth and light against the end-time apostate church system and false religion. Rooted deeply in scriptural foundations, we stand as a beacon of hope and restoration, igniting hearts with a passion for authentic faith. Our mission is to call generations back to uncompromised truth, cultivating a prophetic community driven by love, holiness, and spiritual awakening. Through teaching, prayer, and prophetic insight, we aim to reveal Christ to the world and usher in revival that transforms lives and nations.
